## S001: direct vertex projection (Python feasibility prototype)

- Replace vertex + carrier nonlinear fitting with minimum-norm Newton corrections
  on vertex coordinates. Initial sparse least-squares solver: SciPy LSMR. No
  learned parameters and no truth input. Known boundary positions remain fixed.
- Kawasaki-only projection converges in 3–5 iterations, typically 0.05–0.35s
  on development failures, but produces many Big-Little-Big violations. Rejected
  as a standalone replacement. Blanket angle-family constraints sometimes
  induce major movement or diverge (e.g. Mosquito), confirming earlier warnings.

## S002: crimp relations as exact constraints

- At a blocked local crimp, propose equality between the smallest admissible
  opposite-assignment sector and the current smaller same-assignment sector.
  Track reduced sectors as affine expressions of the original ray angles so
  later crimp steps are covered too. Reproject with Kawasaki as an equality.
- This differs from the old rejected weighted BLB penalty: no tradeoff between
  ordering residuals and Kawasaki precision. The existing Rust checker remains
  the final judge. Read the vendored Check4 and current refined arithmetic.
- Python prototype clears CAMV on Barn Owl, Diablo, Lucanus Bright, and Takuro
  Kashiwamura's Dragon without angle-family constraints (0.13–1.76s). Some
  carrier-fit checks and boundary crimp relations remain. These are exploratory
  timings, concurrent with baseline work, not isolated browser claims.

## S003: shared Rust prototype

- Independent matrix-free LSQR implementation using the Golub–Kahan recurrence;
  reference: https://web.stanford.edu/group/SOL/software/lsqr/ . No new native-only
  dependency, so the formulation is available to WASM. Add open boundary fans.
- First native sweep exposed numerical breakdown on rank-deficient nearly
  solved systems: an inconsistent residual floor led LSQR to take large null-
  space steps. All such results were refused by existing movement checks.
  Add normal-residual stopping and an absolute residual floor; retain failed
  outputs as evidence rather than counting them as successes.
- Carrier bins inferred from noisy geometry also made physically checker-clean
  proposals ambiguous. Re-read geometric bins from the proposed placement,
  as existing E027 does, while retaining explicit source carrier IDs. No change
  to the carrier tolerance or any editor check.
- Baseline processes are sequential per case, but prototype/build work was
  concurrent. Final performance claims require an isolated replay after builds.
