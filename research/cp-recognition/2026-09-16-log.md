### Model/renderer audit

- Read current model history, checkpoint management, July V5 training report,
  synthetic source manifest, boundary target builder, and model implementation.
- CPLineNet predicts heads at stride 4 then bilinearly upsamples. Its ability to
  represent separate narrow junction peaks depends on that coarse lattice.
- The contact target is a Gaussian at the geometric point; side/offset/coord
  regression is supervised only at the single rounded pixel. Thus the September
  bias is not an intentionally displaced GT target, but sparse supervision plus
  the model/rendering behavior. A new target should supervise offsets around
  the whole local neighborhood, with explicit subpixel alignment tests.
- The declared synthetic mix uses generated TreeMaker, Rabbit Ear, tessellation,
  box-pleat, and SEARCH-22.5 patterns. No new trainer has been launched.
- External primary references considered: [DeepLSD](https://github.com/cvg/DeepLSD)
  (distance/angle field plus classical line extraction),
  [HAWP](https://github.com/cherubicXN/hawp) (attraction fields), and
  [LSD](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2012/gjmr-lsd/) (subpixel line extraction).
  These supply architectural alternatives, not evidence of CP performance.
